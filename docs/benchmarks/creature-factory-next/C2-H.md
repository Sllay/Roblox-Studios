# C2-H — semantic landmark contract and independent animal fixture
LandmarkDefinition, LandmarkObservation and LandmarkBundle separate fixed physical surface points, joints and region centers; anatomical left/right is relative to the animal (left +X for the fixture facing -Y), not image side. Each observation is tied to a cropped PNG SHA256, source, visibility and confidence. Missing points remain unknown. Two nonparallel views fit; other visible views are held out. Estimated gauges remain normalized. Unreviewed visual proposals or marker detectors cannot authorize production fusion.
The independent technical quadruped has 15 ellipsoid parts, actual UV PNG textures, exact exported triangles, seven known orthographic cameras, z-buffer depth and normals, painted ID and unmarked natural coat renders. The CPU rasterizer renders the same exported mesh, not a concept approximation. Palette detection is a controlled identity benchmark; it is not learned semantic detection. C1 ground-truth fixtures remain unchanged.
Rabbit candidate bundle contains manually visually located unreviewed landmarks from the original cropped panels, including approximate ear and nose tips and eye/tail regions. Source hashes bind them to the frozen C2-A/B artifact. No real semantic confidence, reviewed identity, metric calibration or reconstruction is claimed.
Initial fixture run 43812195789 passed 12/12 landmarks and 12/12 wrong-identity probes, max 3D error .0621311 units. Final rerun includes explicit anatomical side correction and rabbit proposal diagnostics. C2 remains incomplete until natural-image landmark estimation and independent accuracy are demonstrated. No Roblox publication.
