# P4 world test — valley v8

The test world is deliberately a coherent valley, not the final game. Nine 256-stud playable chunks centered on (0,0), 16 surrounding horizon chunks, total 1280x1280. A single global field provides continuous elevations; existing pilot columns are preserved except the river corridor/bank/road transitions. All columns have a solid foundation at Y=-32. New river starts at (212,300) at Y32 and reaches the original lake at Y20; native water is static, not fluid simulation. Cardinal connectivity checked across 549 wet columns, including 350 river columns. 3667 original pilot columns preserve native voxel geometry. Mountain heights outside play area reach roughly 94 studs, while interior remains a low valley. Boundary recovery only affects living players outside +/-384 or below -40. No invisible floor.

The original Tree_01 is now at (40,28.898504,-18), ground estimate 23.899893, burial 3 studs, rather than the old pit. Existing plants are reanchored or locally relocated where terrain/masks require; new plants clone audited persisted species, preserve relative geometry and PBR references, anchored and noncolliding visual meshes. 138 plants total (33 old, 105 new, including 24 horizon); 57 additional trunk colliders, no horizon collision. 53 planned spawn candidates within radius108. Runtime validates actual five Terrain raycasts, slope, obstruction, and selects a random eligible SpawnLocation, excluding last if possible; never teleports dead characters. Death keeps native joint breaking; torso/head collision is explicitly enabled after death, a hypothesis to validate in client, not a proven fix. P4 panel shows actual FPS, spawn, prop support and corpse observations. Clouds under Terrain, atmosphere haze, water visual settings; original Sky preserved.

Verified run 43147204931: staging v7 -> v8, 25 independent tile digests and 138 model transforms/shape/PBR, 53 spawn instances and trusted scripts persisted; 1759 material-only road voxel changes over 5 tiles, exactly zero solid/liquid changes, max native quantization 0.0039215686.
